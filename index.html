About Publications Projects

Hi! I'm Hannah Sun, a Master's student in Data Science at the University of Washington. I'm involved in two research labs at the UW iSchool: the Lab for Computing Cultural Heritage (L4CCH) and the Language Accessibility Research (LARCH) Lab. I'm also a research fellow in the SPAR program. My research is broadly motivated by how AI systems interact with human knowledge, reasoning, and behavior. In computational humanities, I investigate how modern machine learning methods enable scholars to explore large-scale cultural heritage collections, revealing patterns and relationships that complement traditional close reading. My HCI research studies how AI-assisted interfaces can support comprehension of scientific information without replacing users' own reasoning. More recently, my work with SPAR examines human behavioral phenomena in language models, including how social information and interactions with other agents can influence their judgments and behavior. If our research interests align, I'd love to connect!

I am currently preparing Ph.D. applications for Fall 2027 and look forward to continuing this line of research in graduate study.

SPAR · AI Safety

Social Influence in Language Models

Investigating when and why language models revise their judgments during collaborative problem solving, using counterfactual and mechanistic analyses to study social commitment and psychological reactance.

Independent Study · L4CCH

Recovering Hidden Scientific Labor from Oral Histories: Developing and Evaluating Evidence-Preserving Extraction Methods

Creating a human-annotated benchmark that records who contributed, what they contributed, the type of contribution, the speaker/source of the claim, the supporting transcript evidence, and how explicitly the contribution is expressed. These results will then inform the design of a separate evidence-preserving extraction strategy intended specifically for oral-history testimony.

Advisor: Prof. Benjamin Charles Germain Lee
